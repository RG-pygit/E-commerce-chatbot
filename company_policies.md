# ShopSphere Company Policies

> Synthetic policies created only for this demo RAG project.

## Return Policy
Eligible products may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Products should be in original condition with supplied accessories and proof of purchase. Gift cards and personalized products are non-returnable.

## Refund Policy
Eligible refunds are processed to the original payment method after the returned product is received and inspected.

## Cancellation Policy
Orders may be cancelled before shipment. After shipment, customers may use the return process after delivery, subject to the return policy.

## Delivery Policy
Standard delivery normally takes 3-7 business days. Delivery time can vary by location, availability, holidays, and shipping partner conditions.

## Damaged Product Policy
Customers receiving a damaged product should contact customer support and may be asked for the order number, a description of the damage, and photographs.

## Warranty Policy
Warranty duration is listed with each product. Damage caused by misuse or unauthorized modification is not covered by the standard warranty.

## Payment Policy
Payment must be successfully completed before an order is processed.

## Privacy Policy
Customer information should be used only for legitimate order processing, delivery, account management, and customer support. Sensitive customer information must not be disclosed through the chatbot.
